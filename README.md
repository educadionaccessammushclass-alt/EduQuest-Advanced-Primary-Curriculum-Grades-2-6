# EDUQUEST
## Advanced Primary Curriculum — Grades 2–6

**Developed by Education Access E-Learning Academy**

### 1. Institutional Purpose

EduQuest is an advanced primary education curriculum development programme designed to provide academically rigorous, competency-based and internationally benchmarked learning for learners in Grades 2–6.

The programme is grounded in Tanzania's national curriculum requirements and enriched through internationally recognised educational frameworks and innovative pedagogical approaches.

### 2. Governing Principle

**TIE Alignment as the Foundation.  
International Excellence as the Benchmark.  
Demonstrated Competence as the Ambition.**

EduQuest complements national curriculum requirements through deeper conceptual learning, critical thinking, practical application, creativity and independent problem-solving.

### 3. Programme Scope

- Grade 2
- Grade 3
- Grade 4
- Grade 5
- Grade 6

### 4. Language Policy

English is the primary language of instruction within the programme.

Kiswahili is developed as a separate compulsory subject, with appropriate attention to language proficiency, communication and cultural understanding.

### 5. Academic Standards

EduQuest adopts eight core learning pillars:

1. Foundational Mastery
2. Critical and Analytical Thinking
3. Scientific Inquiry
4. Creativity and Innovation
5. Digital and Technological Literacy
6. Real-World Application
7. Communication and Collaboration
8. Personal Development and Responsible Citizenship

### 6. Curriculum Benchmarking

The curriculum development process references:

- Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE)
- Cambridge Primary
- OECD Learning Compass 2030
- UNESCO educational principles

National curriculum alignment remains mandatory. International frameworks inform enrichment, progression and quality assurance.

### 7. Learning Architecture

Every learning unit will incorporate:

- Discover
- Understand
- Explore
- Practise
- Apply
- Create
- Evaluate
- Master

### 8. Assessment Philosophy

Assessment will measure knowledge, conceptual understanding, practical competence, reasoning, application and independent performance.

Learner progression will be supported through formative assessment, structured practice, summative assessment and evidence of mastery.

### 9. Development Architecture

The repository will contain:

- Curriculum framework
- Grade-specific curricula
- Subject specifications
- Teacher guides
- Learner guides
- Assessment frameworks
- Quality assurance documentation

### 10. Quality Commitment

Every curriculum component must undergo academic review, TIE alignment verification, pedagogical review and assessment validation before publication.
