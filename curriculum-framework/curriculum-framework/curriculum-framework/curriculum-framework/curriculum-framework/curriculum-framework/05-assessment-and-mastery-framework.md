# EDUQUEST PRIMARY CURRICULUM
## ASSESSMENT AND MASTERY FRAMEWORK

**Document ID:** EQ-AMF-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Programme:** EduQuest Advanced Primary Curriculum  
**Coverage:** Grades 2–6  
**Status:** Foundational Framework

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework establishes the assessment, feedback, learner-support and mastery-verification system for EduQuest Grades 2–6.

Assessment shall establish not only what a learner remembers, but also what the learner understands, can explain, can apply and can independently demonstrate.

The framework supports national curriculum alignment while incorporating internationally recognised approaches to formative assessment, competency development and evidence-based learning.

## 2. ASSESSMENT PRINCIPLES

All assessment shall be:

1. Curriculum-aligned and traceable to intended learning outcomes.
2. Age-appropriate and developmentally progressive.
3. Valid, reliable and fair.
4. Inclusive and accessible to learners with different needs.
5. Continuous rather than limited to end-of-term examinations.
6. Evidence-based, with recorded demonstrations of competence.
7. Diagnostic and developmental, identifying both strengths and learning gaps.
8. Constructive, providing actionable feedback.
9. Contextually relevant to Tanzanian learners and their communities.
10. Designed to encourage independent thinking and responsible application.

## 3. THE EDUQUEST ASSESSMENT MODEL

EduQuest shall use eight complementary assessment categories.

### 3.1 Diagnostic Assessment

Purpose:
- Establish prior knowledge.
- Identify misconceptions.
- Determine foundational learning gaps.
- Inform appropriate teaching and support.

Timing:
- At the beginning of a subject, unit or learning sequence.
- Whenever a learner demonstrates persistent difficulty.

Diagnostic results shall inform instruction and shall not automatically be treated as summative grades.

### 3.2 Formative Assessment

Purpose:
- Monitor learning during instruction.
- Identify emerging misconceptions.
- Provide immediate feedback.
- Adjust teaching strategies.

Methods may include:
- Oral questioning.
- Short written exercises.
- Classroom observation.
- Interactive tasks.
- Concept checks.
- Learner explanations.
- Short quizzes.

### 3.3 Guided Practice Assessment

Purpose:
- Establish whether learners can perform tasks with structured assistance.
- Identify the level of scaffolding required.
- Develop confidence and procedural accuracy.

Teachers shall gradually reduce assistance as competence develops.

### 3.4 Independent Practice Assessment

Purpose:
- Establish whether learners can complete tasks without direct assistance.
- Measure accuracy, understanding and consistency.
- Determine readiness for more advanced work.

### 3.5 Practical and Applied Assessment

Purpose:
- Test the application of knowledge in realistic situations.
- Connect classroom learning with everyday experience.
- Assess practical competence.

Examples:
- Measuring objects and distances.
- Conducting simple scientific investigations.
- Interpreting information from local environments.
- Planning a basic budget.
- Producing written reports.
- Designing practical solutions to community problems.

### 3.6 Project-Based Assessment

Purpose:
- Assess sustained application of learning.
- Develop research, creativity and collaboration.
- Encourage interdisciplinary thinking.

Projects may include:
- Environmental investigations.
- Mathematics and market-price projects.
- Local history investigations.
- Science models.
- Creative design challenges.
- Community improvement proposals.

Projects shall have clear assessment criteria and distinguish individual achievement from group performance.

### 3.7 Summative Assessment

Purpose:
- Evaluate achievement after a defined learning period.
- Establish the extent to which intended outcomes have been achieved.
- Inform reporting and future instructional planning.

Methods may include:
- Topic tests.
- Structured written assessments.
- Practical examinations.
- End-of-unit assessments.
- Term assessments.
- End-of-year assessments.

Summative assessment shall not be the sole basis for determining learner competence.

### 3.8 Reflective Assessment

Purpose:
- Develop learner self-awareness.
- Encourage responsibility for learning.
- Support identification of strengths and improvement priorities.

Learners shall progressively learn to:
- Explain what they have learned.
- Identify what they found difficult.
- Review mistakes.
- Set improvement targets.
- Evaluate their own work against clear criteria.

## 4. THE EDUQUEST EIGHT-STAGE EVIDENCE CYCLE

Learning evidence shall be gathered through the following stages:

1. Discover — establish prior knowledge and curiosity.
2. Understand — check conceptual comprehension.
3. Explore — assess observation, investigation and reasoning.
4. Practise — assess developing accuracy and fluency.
5. Apply — assess transfer of learning to new situations.
6. Create — assess originality, design and production.
7. Evaluate — assess reasoning, reflection and quality of work.
8. Master — verify independent and sufficiently consistent competence.

The stages may be adapted to suit the subject and learning outcome.

## 5. MASTERY CLASSIFICATION

EduQuest shall use the following internal mastery classifications.

| Classification | Meaning | Instructional response |
|---|---|---|
| Beginning | Foundational understanding is not yet secure | Re-teaching and targeted support |
| Developing | Partial understanding with continuing assistance required | Guided practice and feedback |
| Approaching Mastery | Most essential elements are demonstrated, but consistency remains incomplete | Focused practice and correction |
| Mastered | The learner demonstrates the intended outcome independently and consistently | Progress to extension or the next learning objective |
| Advanced Application | The learner transfers learning to unfamiliar or more complex situations | Enrichment and higher-order challenges |

A classification shall be supported by relevant evidence, not a single isolated response.

## 6. PROVISIONAL INTERNAL MASTERY THRESHOLD

EduQuest may use an initial benchmark of 80% for designated knowledge-based assessments.

This is an internal programme design benchmark, not:
- An official TIE grading scale.
- A national examination grade.
- An automatic promotion requirement.
- A universal measure of practical or creative competence.

An 80% score shall not automatically establish mastery where the learner cannot explain or apply the knowledge.

Practical, oral, creative and performance-based outcomes shall use appropriate subject-specific rubrics.

The threshold shall be reviewed through pilot implementation and assessment validation.

## 7. ASSESSMENT RUBRICS

Each significant assessment shall have transparent criteria.

Depending on the task, criteria may include:

- Accuracy.
- Conceptual understanding.
- Reasoning.
- Method and procedure.
- Communication.
- Application.
- Creativity.
- Independence.
- Quality of evidence.
- Reflection and improvement.

Rubrics shall describe observable performance rather than rely on vague judgments.

## 8. GRADE-LEVEL ASSESSMENT PROGRESSION

### Grade 2
Emphasis:
- Foundational literacy and numeracy.
- Oral explanation.
- Recognition and understanding.
- Concrete demonstrations.
- Short, age-appropriate tasks.
- Observation and guided practice.

### Grade 3
Emphasis:
- Improved reading and written expression.
- More secure numerical operations.
- Explanation of simple reasoning.
- Guided investigations.
- Structured independent tasks.

### Grade 4
Emphasis:
- Conceptual understanding.
- Multi-step problem-solving.
- Interpretation of information.
- More developed written responses.
- Scientific observation and explanation.

### Grade 5
Emphasis:
- Analytical reasoning.
- Independent application.
- Research and project work.
- Interpretation of evidence.
- More complex problem-solving.

### Grade 6
Emphasis:
- Consolidated primary-level competence.
- Independent reasoning.
- Extended written communication.
- Interdisciplinary application.
- Research, evaluation and presentation.
- Preparation for secondary-level learning.

These expectations shall be validated against the applicable national grade-level requirements.

## 9. LEARNER SUPPORT AND REMEDIATION

Assessment must lead to an instructional response.

Where a learner has not mastered an outcome, the programme shall:

1. Identify the specific learning difficulty.
2. Determine whether the difficulty concerns prior knowledge, language, conceptual understanding or task execution.
3. Provide targeted teaching.
4. Offer alternative explanations and learning activities.
5. Provide additional guided practice.
6. Reassess the same outcome using suitable evidence.
7. Record progress and remaining needs.

Repeated assessment without appropriate instructional intervention shall not be considered sufficient remediation.

## 10. ENRICHMENT AND ADVANCED LEARNING

Learners demonstrating secure mastery shall receive opportunities for further development.

Enrichment may include:
- Open-ended problems.
- Advanced reading.
- Independent research.
- Scientific investigations.
- Creative production.
- Mathematical challenges.
- Interdisciplinary projects.
- Community-based learning.

Enrichment shall deepen understanding rather than merely increase the quantity of routine exercises.

## 11. DIGITAL ASSESSMENT AND LEARNING EVIDENCE

The EduQuest digital platform should progressively support:

- Learner assessment records.
- Diagnostic results.
- Formative assessment history.
- Automated marking for suitable objective questions.
- Teacher-marked responses.
- Practical assessment rubrics.
- Project evidence.
- Mastery status by learning outcome.
- Feedback and remediation records.
- Learner progress reports.
- Secure teacher and administrator access.

Automated assessment shall not replace professional teacher judgment for complex reasoning, written expression, practical work or creative performance.

## 12. LEARNER PORTFOLIOS

Each learner should progressively develop an evidence portfolio containing selected examples of:

- Written work.
- Mathematics solutions.
- Scientific investigations.
- Creative projects.
- Research assignments.
- Oral presentation records.
- Reflective entries.
- Assessment feedback.
- Demonstrations of improvement.

Portfolios shall demonstrate development over time rather than simply accumulate completed exercises.

## 13. REPORTING AND COMMUNICATION

Learner reports should communicate:

1. Learning outcomes achieved.
2. Demonstrated strengths.
3. Areas requiring improvement.
4. Evidence of practical application.
5. Learning behaviours and participation.
6. Remediation undertaken.
7. Recommended next learning priorities.

Reports should distinguish achievement from effort and participation.

## 14. ASSESSMENT QUALITY ASSURANCE

EduQuest shall implement the following quality controls:

- Alignment between assessment and curriculum outcomes.
- Moderation of selected teacher-marked assessments.
- Review of assessment difficulty and age appropriateness.
- Monitoring of marking consistency.
- Review of learner performance patterns.
- Identification of possible assessment bias.
- Verification of mastery evidence.
- Periodic revision of assessment instruments.

Assessment records shall be handled securely and accessed only by authorised persons.

## 15. NATIONAL ALIGNMENT AND REGULATORY STATUS

All assessment arrangements shall be reviewed against applicable Tanzanian Institute of Education curriculum and assessment requirements.

EduQuest internal mastery classifications shall remain distinct from official national grading, examination and certification arrangements.

Where official national requirements prescribe particular assessment methods or reporting procedures, those requirements shall take precedence.

## 16. IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITIES

The framework shall be implemented progressively:

**Phase 1:** Define learning outcomes and assessment criteria.

**Phase 2:** Develop grade- and subject-specific assessment instruments.

**Phase 3:** Establish diagnostic and formative assessment routines.

**Phase 4:** Introduce mastery tracking and learner portfolios.

**Phase 5:** Pilot assessment tools and moderate results.

**Phase 6:** Review effectiveness and improve the system.
