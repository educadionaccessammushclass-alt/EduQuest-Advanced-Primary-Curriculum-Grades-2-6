**National Reference Authority:** Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE)  
**Institution:** Education Access E-Learning Academy

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This document establishes the national curriculum alignment and verification framework for the EduQuest Advanced Primary Curriculum.

Its purpose is to ensure that every subject, learning outcome, teaching resource and assessment component is traceable to the applicable Tanzanian national curriculum requirements.

EduQuest shall enrich national curriculum provision without omitting or misrepresenting prescribed learning requirements.

## 2. NATIONAL REFERENCE DOCUMENTS

The curriculum development team shall obtain and maintain the applicable official TIE documents, including:

1. Primary Education Curriculum, 2023.
2. Standard I–II syllabus and applicable subject requirements.
3. Standard III–VI syllabus documents.
4. Applicable subject-specific syllabi.
5. Relevant national assessment and education policy documents.

Only verified official documents shall be used as authoritative alignment references.

## 3. ALIGNMENT LEVELS

Every EduQuest learning outcome shall be assigned one of the following classifications:

### A. Direct National Alignment

The learning outcome directly corresponds to a prescribed national curriculum requirement.

### B. National Curriculum Enrichment

The learning outcome extends a prescribed requirement through additional depth, practice or application.

### C. International Benchmark Enrichment

The learning outcome introduces additional learning informed by recognised international educational frameworks.

### D. EduQuest Innovation

The learning outcome develops additional competencies, including digital literacy, interdisciplinary thinking and creative problem-solving.

Additional classifications must never be used to disguise missing national curriculum requirements.

## 5. LEARNING OUTCOME TRACEABILITY

Every curriculum learning outcome shall have a unique identifier.

Recommended identification format:

EQ-[GRADE]-[SUBJECT]-[DOMAIN]-[NUMBER]

Example:

EQ-G3-MATH-NUM-001

Each outcome shall be linked to:

- Official TIE syllabus reference.
- National learning requirement.
- EduQuest learning outcome.
- Teaching and learning activities.
- Assessment task.
- Evidence of achievement.
- Enrichment classification.

## 6. ALIGNMENT VERIFICATION PROCEDURE

The curriculum development team shall:

1. Obtain the official TIE syllabus.
2. Identify prescribed competencies and learning outcomes.
3. Extract the relevant subject content.
4. Map national requirements to EduQuest outcomes.
5. Identify omissions and potential duplication.
6. Develop appropriate enrichment.
7. Verify assessment coverage.
8. Record documentary evidence.
9. Conduct academic review.
10. Approve the completed alignment register.

## 7. ALIGNMENT STATUS DEFINITIONS

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Verification has not started |
| Under Review | Documentary comparison underway |
| Aligned | National requirement verified and mapped |
| Enriched | National requirement covered with additional depth |
| Gap Identified | Required content or competency missing |
| Approved | Academic and alignment review completed |

## 8. QUALITY CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

No grade or subject package shall receive final curriculum approval until:

- All applicable national learning requirements have been identified.
- Every prescribed outcome has been mapped.
- Content coverage has been verified.
- Assessment coverage has been established.
- Identified gaps have been resolved.
- Documentary references have been recorded.

## 9. INTERNATIONAL BENCHMARKING

International frameworks shall inform additional curriculum development through:

- Cambridge Primary learning progression.
- OECD Learning Compass 2030.
- UNESCO education principles.

International benchmarking shall complement national alignment and shall not be represented as formal TIE approval.

## 10. DOCUMENT CONTROL

The alignment matrix shall be updated whenever:

- National curriculum documents are revised.
- New subject requirements are identified.
- Learning outcomes are modified.
- Assessment specifications change.
- Academic review identifies a compliance gap.

All changes shall be version-controlled.
