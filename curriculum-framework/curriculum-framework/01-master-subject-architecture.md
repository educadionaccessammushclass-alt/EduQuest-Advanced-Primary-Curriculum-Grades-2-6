# EDUQUEST MASTER SUBJECT ARCHITECTURE

**Document ID:** EQ-MSA-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Coverage:** Grades 2–6  
**Institution:** Education Access E-Learning Academy

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This document establishes the subject architecture for EduQuest Grades 2–6.

It provides a coordinated structure for subject development, interdisciplinary learning, academic progression and curriculum integration.

The architecture shall be validated against the applicable TIE syllabus for each grade before subject packages are finalised.

## 2. CORE SUBJECT DEVELOPMENT AREAS

### 2.1 Mathematics

Core domains:

- Numbers and operations.
- Fractions and decimals.
- Measurement.
- Geometry.
- Patterns and algebraic thinking.
- Statistics and data handling.
- Mathematical reasoning.
- Financial mathematics.
- Applied problem-solving.

### 2.2 English Language

Core domains:

- Listening and speaking.
- Phonics and pronunciation.
- Vocabulary development.
- Reading fluency.
- Reading comprehension.
- Grammar and language structures.
- Creative writing.
- Functional writing.
- Oral presentation.
- Critical reading.

### 2.3 Kiswahili Language

Core domains:

- Kusikiliza na kuzungumza.
- Kusoma.
- Kuandika.
- Msamiati.
- Sarufi.
- Ufahamu.
- Fasihi na ubunifu wa lugha.
- Mawasiliano katika mazingira mbalimbali.

### 2.4 Science

Core domains:

- Living things.
- Human body and health.
- Plants and animals.
- Matter and materials.
- Energy.
- Forces and motion.
- Earth and environment.
- Weather and climate.
- Scientific investigation.
- Technology and innovation.

### 2.5 Social Studies and Citizenship

Core domains:

- Family and community.
- Tanzanian history.
- Geography.
- Culture and heritage.
- Citizenship.
- Environmental responsibility.
- Economic activities.
- Community development.
- African and global awareness.

The precise division of these domains into TIE-prescribed subjects shall follow the relevant grade syllabus.

### 2.6 Creative Arts and Design

Core domains:

- Visual arts.
- Drawing and painting.
- Music.
- Movement and performance.
- Handcraft.
- Design thinking.
- Creative expression.

### 2.7 Physical Education and Health

Core domains:

- Physical fitness.
- Movement skills.
- Games and sports.
- Personal hygiene.
- Nutrition.
- Safety.
- Healthy living.
- Teamwork.

### 2.8 Digital Literacy and Computational Thinking

Core domains:

- Digital device awareness.
- Responsible technology use.
- Information literacy.
- Digital communication.
- Logical reasoning.
- Sequencing and algorithms.
- Problem decomposition.
- Age-appropriate computational activities.
- Digital safety.

Digital literacy shall enrich the prescribed curriculum and shall not be presented as an officially prescribed standalone subject unless confirmed by the applicable national syllabus.

### 2.9 Values, Ethics and Personal Development

Core domains:

- Respect and responsibility.
- Honesty and integrity.
- Self-management.
- Cooperation.
- Cultural understanding.
- Ethical reasoning.
- Financial awareness.
- Environmental stewardship.

## 3. GRADE-LEVEL DEVELOPMENT

### Grade 2

Emphasis:
- Foundational literacy and numeracy.
- Concrete learning experiences.
- Basic observation and classification.
- Communication and expression.
- Everyday problem-solving.

### Grade 3

Emphasis:
- Expanding foundational knowledge.
- Guided investigation.
- Reading comprehension.
- Mathematical reasoning.
- Structured communication.

### Grade 4

Emphasis:
- Conceptual depth.
- Independent application.
- Data interpretation.
- Scientific investigation.
- Analytical thinking.

### Grade 5

Emphasis:
- Multi-step problem-solving.
- Research and evidence.
- Extended writing.
- Practical projects.
- Interdisciplinary understanding.

### Grade 6

Emphasis:
- Consolidated primary competence.
- Independent investigation.
- Advanced primary-level reasoning.
- Synthesis of knowledge.
- Preparation for secondary education.

## 4. INTERDISCIPLINARY LEARNING

Subjects shall be connected through meaningful themes.

Examples:

- Mathematics and agriculture.
- Science and environmental conservation.
- English and historical research.
- Kiswahili and cultural heritage.
- Geography and climate.
- Mathematics and financial literacy.
- Digital literacy and scientific investigation.

Interdisciplinary activities must reinforce, rather than replace, subject-specific learning outcomes.

## 5. CURRICULUM DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Each subject package shall include:

1. Subject specification.
2. Grade-level learning outcomes.
3. Content sequence.
4. Key concepts and vocabulary.
5. Teaching guidance.
6. Learner activities.
7. Guided practice.
8. Independent practice.
9. Application activities.
10. Assessment framework.
11. Mastery criteria.
12. Differentiated learning support.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION PRINCIPLE

All subject packages shall be developed independently, reviewed academically and subsequently integrated into the EduQuest master curriculum.

National alignment shall be verified against official TIE documentation before approval.
