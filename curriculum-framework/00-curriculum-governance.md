## 1. PURPOSE

This document establishes the academic governance principles, curriculum development standards and quality assurance requirements governing the EduQuest Advanced Primary Curriculum.

It applies to all subjects, grades, learning materials, assessments, teacher resources and digital learning components developed under EduQuest.

## 2. CURRICULUM AUTHORITY

EduQuest operates through three complementary levels:

### 2.1 National Curriculum Alignment

The Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE) curriculum and applicable national education requirements constitute the mandatory reference for curriculum coverage and compliance.

### 2.2 International Academic Benchmarking

International frameworks inform curriculum enrichment, academic progression and quality assurance.

Reference frameworks include:

- Cambridge Primary.
- OECD Learning Compass 2030.
- UNESCO education and learning principles.

### 2.3 EduQuest Academic Enrichment

EduQuest develops additional learning opportunities through:

- Conceptual depth.
- Critical thinking.
- Scientific investigation.
- Independent research.
- Creative production.
- Practical problem-solving.
- Digital literacy.
- Interdisciplinary learning.

Enrichment must not compromise prescribed national learning requirements.

## 3. CURRICULUM DEVELOPMENT SCOPE

The programme covers:

- Grade 2.
- Grade 3.
- Grade 4.
- Grade 5.
- Grade 6.

Each grade shall have independently documented learning outcomes, subject content, instructional guidance and assessment specifications.

## 4. LANGUAGE OF INSTRUCTION

English shall be the primary language of instruction within the EduQuest programme.

Kiswahili shall be developed as a separate compulsory subject.

Language requirements shall be reviewed against applicable Tanzanian national education and examination requirements before implementation.

## 5. ACADEMIC DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES

All curriculum materials shall demonstrate:

1. Accuracy and intellectual integrity.
2. Age-appropriate academic progression.
3. Conceptual understanding.
4. Competency-based learning.
5. Practical application.
6. Inclusive educational design.
7. Cultural and contextual relevance.
8. Measurable learning outcomes.

## 6. THE EDUQUEST LEARNING CYCLE

Every learning unit shall use the following instructional sequence:

1. Discover.
2. Understand.
3. Explore.
4. Practise.
5. Apply.
6. Create.
7. Evaluate.
8. Master.

Individual subjects may adapt the sequence where pedagogically justified.

## 7. LEARNING OUTCOME REQUIREMENTS

Every subject and grade shall specify:

- General learning objectives.
- Specific learning outcomes.
- Essential knowledge.
- Required competencies.
- Key terminology.
- Learning activities.
- Practical applications.
- Assessment criteria.
- Evidence of mastery.

Learning outcomes must be observable, assessable and appropriate to the learner's developmental stage.

## 8. ASSESSMENT GOVERNANCE

Assessment shall include:

- Diagnostic assessment.
- Formative assessment.
- Guided practice.
- Independent practice.
- Practical assessment.
- Periodic summative assessment.
- Reflective assessment.
- Evidence-based mastery verification.

An 80% threshold may be adopted as a provisional EduQuest mastery benchmark for designated knowledge-based outcomes. It shall not automatically determine progression or replace official national grading requirements.

## 9. INCLUSION AND DIFFERENTIATION

Curriculum design shall accommodate:

- Different learning abilities.
- Different learning speeds.
- Learners requiring additional support.
- Advanced and gifted learners.
- Diverse linguistic backgrounds.
- Accessible learning materials.

Every subject shall provide appropriate opportunities for remediation and academic extension.

## 10. QUALITY ASSURANCE

No curriculum component shall be approved for publication without review of:

1. National curriculum alignment.
2. Academic accuracy.
3. Pedagogical suitability.
4. Learning outcome coverage.
5. Assessment validity.
6. Language quality.
7. Accessibility and inclusion.
8. Digital functionality where applicable.

## 11. DOCUMENT CONTROL

All curriculum documents shall have:

- Unique document identifiers.
- Version numbers.
- Publication dates.
- Defined grade and subject coverage.
- Review and approval status.

Changes to approved curriculum materials shall be documented and version-controlled.

## 12. GOVERNANCE COMMITMENT

EduQuest is committed to developing learners who can acquire knowledge, understand concepts, reason critically, communicate effectively, investigate problems and apply learning independently.

Its governing ambition is to establish a rigorous, relevant and internationally benchmarked primary learning programme grounded in Tanzania's national educational context.
